DVI-YANTRA — System Architecture

1. Project Overview

DVI-YANTRA is a proposed CO₂ separation research project exploring a sugarcane bagasse-derived cellulose membrane coated with Zeolite 4A.

The project aims to investigate whether the membrane can provide useful CO₂ transport or separation performance. Experimental performance has not yet been established.

2. Proposed System Components

- Membrane: Sugarcane bagasse-derived cellulose membrane coated with Zeolite 4A.
- Membrane holder: Supports the membrane and helps prevent bypass leakage.
- Test-cell housing: Holds the membrane assembly.
- Gas supply and flow controls: To be selected and checked by a qualified laboratory supervisor.
- Measurement system: Appropriate gas-flow and composition measurements, depending on the approved test setup.

3. Conceptual Flow

1. Prepare and characterize the membrane.
2. Install it in a suitable test cell using a compatible holder and gasket.
3. Introduce the test gas using laboratory-approved equipment.
4. Measure gas flow and composition at the appropriate outlets.
5. Record test conditions and repeat measurements.
6. Analyze the results before drawing conclusions about separation performance.

4. Preliminary Design

A conceptual housing size of approximately 50 mm internal diameter and 100 mm length has been considered for discussion. These are preliminary design estimates, not validated dimensions.

The final membrane area, holder geometry, seals, fittings, flow path, and operating conditions require laboratory review and engineering verification.

This concept is not a certified pressure vessel or an approved operating procedure.

5. Validation Plan

Planned characterization may include:

- SEM for membrane surface and morphology.
- XRD and FTIR for material characterization.
- TGA for thermal characterization.
- Suitable gas permeation tests to measure flow and calculate permeability.
- CO₂/N₂ mixture testing to evaluate separation performance, if appropriate equipment is available.

Testing with pure CO₂ alone cannot establish CO₂/N₂ selectivity.

6. Current Status

Stage: Concept development and test planning.

No experimental separation results or validated performance claims are reported in this repository yet.

7. Safety

All gas testing must be reviewed and supervised by qualified laboratory personnel. Use suitable certified regulators, fittings, and approved equipment. Do not operate an improvised or unverified pressurized assembly.
