DVI-YANTRA — Membrane Testing Plan

1. Objective

To investigate whether a sugarcane bagasse-derived cellulose membrane coated with Zeolite 4A shows useful carbon dioxide (CO₂) transport or separation performance.

2. Proposed Membrane Structure

- Base layer: Cellulose membrane derived from sugarcane bagasse.
- Coating: Zeolite 4A intended to cover the upper surface of the cellulose membrane.
- Support: A suitable porous support and compatible edge seal to hold the membrane in place.
- Test cell: A laboratory-approved flat-membrane test cell.

The coating, membrane integrity, and final test-cell arrangement must be verified experimentally.

3. Planned Tests

Test 1 — Visual Inspection

Inspect the membrane for cracks, uneven coating, visible gaps, and damage. Record photographs and observations.

Test 2 — Material Characterization

Where facilities are available, use:

- SEM: Examine surface morphology and coating coverage.
- XRD: Investigate crystalline phases and evidence consistent with Zeolite 4A.
- FTIR: Examine characteristic chemical bonds and functional groups.
- TGA: Assess thermal behaviour of the membrane material.
- BET: Porous size and surface area calculation

These methods are planned; no results are claimed at this stage.

Test 3 — Gas Transport Measurement

Use a laboratory-approved membrane test system with calibrated flow and pressure measurement. Record the test conditions and measured gas flow or permeation data.

Testing must be supervised by qualified laboratory personnel using appropriate certified equipment.

Test 4 — CO₂/N₂ Separation Evaluation

A pure CO₂ test can provide preliminary CO₂ transport information, but it cannot establish CO₂/N₂ selectivity.

To evaluate separation, conduct an approved mixed CO₂/N₂ feed test and measure the gas composition on the relevant outlet streams using a suitable analyzer. Calculate selectivity only when the required measurements are available.

4. Data to Record

For each test, record:

- Membrane sample identification and preparation details
- Feed-gas composition
- Temperature and approved operating conditions
- Calibrated flow and pressure readings
- Outlet gas composition, where measured
- Test duration, repeat measurements, and observations

5. Current Status

Status: Planned — experimental validation pending.

No experimental results, measured permeance, separation selectivity, or capture efficiency are claimed in this repository yet.

6. Safety

All gas tests must be reviewed and supervised by qualified laboratory personnel. Use an approved test system, suitable certified regulators and fittings, appropriate ventilation, and an approved exhaust arrangement. Do not operate an improvised or unverified pressurized assembly.

7. Future Work

1. Prepare and characterize membrane samples.
2. Verify coating coverage and membrane integrity.
3. Complete supervised gas-transport measurements.
4. Conduct mixed-gas separation tests if the approved setup permits.
5. Report measured results, limitations, and repeatability transparently.
