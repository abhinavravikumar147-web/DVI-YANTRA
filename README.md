# DVI-YANTRA
Bagasse-derived cellulose membrane coated with Zeolite 4A for CO2 capture research and prototyping.
DVI-YANTRA

Bagasse-Derived Cellulose Membrane Coated with Zeolite 4A for CO₂ Capture Research

1. Problem Statement

Carbon dioxide emissions contribute to climate change. This project explores a potential sustainable material approach using sugarcane bagasse, an agricultural by-product, to develop a cellulose-based membrane coated with Zeolite 4A.

2. Proposed Solution

The proposed prototype consists of:

- A stainless-steel test column
- A flat, circular cellulose membrane derived from sugarcane bagasse
- A Zeolite 4A coating
- Suitable membrane support and gas connections
- CO₂ measurement equipment for laboratory testing

3. Objectives

- Explore the use of agricultural waste in sustainable materials.
- Develop a cellulose–Zeolite 4A composite membrane.
- Evaluate its behavior during supervised CO₂ testing.
- Investigate its potential for future carbon capture applications.

4. Prototype Status

Current stage: Design and prototype development.

Experimental performance, CO₂ capture capacity, membrane durability, and gas separation selectivity have not yet been established. Results will be added only after testing.

5. Proposed Testing

The planned test will use a laboratory-approved CO₂ supply and suitable measurement equipment. The test conditions, instrument readings, observations, and limitations will be documented.

6. Safety

All gas testing will be conducted under qualified laboratory supervision using compatible equipment and appropriate ventilation. No unverified pressure vessel or improvised cylinder connection will be used.
